## Booking an appointment

Choose a video appointment on [Doctolib](https://www.doctolib.fr/psychologue/l-etang-sale/benedicte-donet). For questions before booking, you can [email me](mailto:donetbenedicte@gmail.com).

-   **Duration —** Sessions last **1 hour 30 minutes**, so you have time to share, settle in, and work through what you need.
-   **Pace —** The number of sessions depends on what you wish to work on and on your sensitivity.
-   **Format —** Teleconsultation only. The address on Google is not that of a practice.
-   **Availability —** Check the appointments currently offered on Doctolib. If none suits you, email me to discuss possible support or a referral.

[Book my session](https://www.doctolib.fr/psychologue/l-etang-sale/benedicte-donet)

## Your psychologist and the consultation framework

I hold a **master’s degree in clinical psychology and psychopathology** from Paul-Valéry University, Montpellier (2015), and completed EMDR training in Australia with [EMDRAA](https://emdraa.org/) in 2022. Explore [my professional background](/en/a-propos/) and [how consultations work](/en/psychotherapie/).

Sessions are **individual**, mainly for adults. For an adolescent aged 13 or over who personally wishes to attend, please email me before booking.

Appointments are booked on **Doctolib**. Instructions for joining your video session are provided before the appointment. The address in L’Étang-Salé, Réunion, is administrative only; no consultations take place there.

## What to know before booking

-   I do not participate in the French « Mon soutien psy » scheme. You therefore cannot be reimbursed for sessions under this specific programme.
-   However, outside of this scheme, some **health insurance providers** may offer reimbursement for a certain number of sessions. Do not hesitate to contact your insurer to ask. If they offer reimbursements, please let me know in advance so that I can send you an invoice for each of our sessions.
-   Whatever your sexual orientation and/or gender identity, you are welcome. I sensitise myself and train as much as possible to offer a **safe and welcoming space** for all. I am also open to evolving and learning from my patients.

For any other questions, I will be happy to answer you by [email](mailto:donetbenedicte@gmail.com).
